# Changelog

## Build 64 (v0.59.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 64: steam and rails.
- Railways (the Railways study, Industrial age): a steel mill makes steel from iron and coal, a rolling mill makes rails from steel, and a locomotive works builds locomotives.
- Lay track with the Rail tool (on the toolbar once Railways is studied): from a railway station or rail to where it should go, one rail a tile, three a tile on a bridge across water. Rails cross roads at level crossings; "No rail" takes a stretch up again (half the rails come back).
- Railway stations are small stores on the track. Two stations on the same track make a line. Every locomotive in your stores is a train: it carries up to 40 goods from a station with plenty to one with little, about four times as fast as a cart, for 1 coal a trip. You see the engine, its smoke and its wagons with their loads. A station's info shows its line, the trains and the goods they carried.
- Enemy columns marching over a line tear up a few tiles of it.
- Steam engines: with the Steam engine study, sawmills, mills, bakeries, breweries, weavers, brickworks, smelters, smithies and other workshops can take a steam engine (4 iron, 6 bricks, 2 steel; fitted in 40 seconds while they keep working). They then work 60% faster for 1 coal every 2 work cycles, with a brick chimney and smoke; without coal they work at the old speed. The smoke makes homes within 4 tiles a little less content.
- Rivals build steel, fit steam engines to their busiest workshops and lay a railway between two of their stores.

## Build 63 (v0.58.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 63: dressed for the age, music for the later ages, voices, goals.
- People wear their age's hats: straw hats in Roman times, then hoods, feathered berets, tricorns, top hats and flat caps, on workers, porters and builders (when zoomed in near enough to see).
- Music for the later ages: two new pieces, a stately court dance (from the Renaissance) and a march over the works (from the Industrial age), join the calm music as your realm moves on.
- Small voices: a word from the worker when you pick one of your buildings: low for soldiers, brighter for artisans (Settings can switch them off).
- Sandbox goals: in endless games, 24 goals one after another, from a first village of 30 people to all five wonders, each with some gold from the treasury. They show where the first-game guide was, with how far you are and what comes next (Settings: "Goals to aim for in endless games").
- Fixed: a geologist's markers on sulfur and lead deposits stopped the deposits' marks from being drawn.

## Build 62 (v0.57.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 62: guilds, a build queue, copying settings.
- Guilds: with the Guilds tech, three or more working buildings of one trade within 8 tiles of each other form a guild and work 10% faster (15% with five or more). A building's info says whether it is in a guild, or how many more it needs.
- The build queue: a building site's info has "Add to the build queue" (or point at a site and press Shift+Q). Queued sites are built one after another, in the order you queued them, before any other site; the rest of the queue waits its turn. Their numbers show over them on the map, and a waiting site says so.
- Copy settings: a building's info has "Copy settings to all N": every other building of its kind gets the same priority, inputs to keep, crop (farms), or soldiers wanted and archers (military buildings).

## Build 61 (v0.56.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 61: trade worth doing, standing orders, the realms graph.
- The Market window (M) sells and buys in lots of 50 as well as 10, so cheap goods like planks and stone are worth a trip.
- Standing orders at the market: sell or buy a good every 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes, keeping a reserve (sell only while more than it stays; buy only up to it). The window lists each order with how much went and the gold it made or cost, and why it waited last time. The log says what went through.
- A sale no longer loses gold the stores have no room for: it waits until there is room.
- The realms graph: a Realms button (and V) opens the Statistics window on every realm compared over time, now with gold and wealth (goods and gold, in gold), and a gold column in the table.

## Build 60 (v0.55.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 60: town quarters and rivals that plan towns.
- Town quarters: homes within reach of a working tavern, a working chapel and a market form a quarter. Their people are more content, and the realm's nobles pay up to 25% more tax (by the share of homes in quarters). A home's info says whether it is in one; a market's info counts its quarter's homes; the side panel shows the share.

- Rivals plan their towns: the tavern, chapel and market go next to each other as a town centre, and new homes go in a ring round it.
- Rivals build a market once their town has grown, buy the goods their workshops stand idle for (when the price is fair), and put boats on rivers: two river landings on the same water when their land has rivers or lakes.

## Build 59 (v0.54.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 59: the coast defended, and a faster late game.
- Sails off the coast: when an enemy landing comes within sight of the building it makes for, you are told while it is still at sea, with about how long until it lands.

## Build 58 (v0.53.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 58: blueprints.
- Blueprints (U, or Blueprints on the top bar): copy a group of your buildings and the roads between them from the map (click two corners), name it and pick a category. Blueprints are kept for every game.

- Trees or roads in the way: you are asked whether to clear the way and build. The same question now comes when a single building can't go only because trees or a road stand on its plot.

- Fixed: a test map put a tower off the edge of the map.

## Build 57 (v0.52.0, 2026-11-15)

Build 57: gunpowder and lead.
- New resources: sulfur (from sulfur mines on new yellow deposits), saltpeter (saltpeter works), charcoal (charcoal burner), and gunpowder from the powder mill (1 sulfur, 2 saltpeter, 1 charcoal make 2 gunpowder).
- Lead: lead mines on new grey-blue deposits, a lead smelter turns lead ore and coal into lead, and the cartridge works makes cartridges from lead and gunpowder.
- Guns need powder: cannon forts (bastion, battery, redoubt) and coastal forts fire only while they have gunpowder, and use it per volley. The naval yard needs gunpowder for its warships. A gunsmith makes firearms from iron, planks and powder.
- Musketeers and riflemen need cartridges: a column packs them when it marches, and each volley uses them; without cartridges they fight hand to hand. Defenders take theirs from your stores.
- Blasting powder (research): mines work 30% faster while you keep 4 or more gunpowder in store; each work cycle uses one.
- Fireworks from the pyrotechnist: a festival with fireworks lasts half as long again and speeds work more.
- Rivals build the whole powder and lead chain when they need guns.
- Fixed: a saved game could go a little differently after loading when the season turned right at the moment it was saved.
- Fixed: a building spot next to water could look reachable for a road through a bridge ending on another building's wall; no road could join it.
- Fixed: the map editor's fairness check stopped on maps with the new deposits.

## Build 52 (v0.47.0, 2026-11-14)

Build 52: warships, coastal forts, field depots, generals.
- Warships: a naval yard on the coast (after the navigators' school) builds them from planks, iron and weapons, up to 8. They guard the water near your harbours, chase enemy warships, fire on landing fleets coming at your coast and sink enemy cargo ships. Each takes three hits.

## Build 47 (v0.42.0, 2026-11-14)

## Build 10 (v0.9.0, 2026-11-07)

- Smarter road laying: a new building's road now joins the network where the new road plus the walk on to a store is shortest, not simply at the nearest road. Carriers walk about 13% less on average (3.90 s to 3.40 s from a working building to its store in a 30-minute 6-realm test) for about 2% more road.
- Faster road network: new roads, buildings, finished stores and paving update the network in place instead of rebuilding it; only losses rebuild it, and a border change rebuilds only realms that lost road or building tiles. ## Build 8 (v0.8.0, 2026-11-07)

- Demolish asks first: a window lists what you get back, what is lost (goods waiting for carriers, stored goods above the new limit, your border) and what happens to the worker and soldiers.
